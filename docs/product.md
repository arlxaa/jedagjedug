# Product — Club Lights

A playful, English-language web app that turns any phone, tablet, or laptop
screen into a club light show. Connect Spotify (Premium), hit "Lights on", and
the screen produces automatically generated, music-reactive visuals — no manual
controls, no uploaded audio.

## Who it's for
Anyone who wants an instant, zero-setup "clubbing on your device" moment —
parties, pre-games, bored evenings, kids dancing in the living room.

## What makes it good
- Lights are 100% auto-generated from live audio analysis. The user never
  programs anything.
- Instant and playful: sticker-book meets glow-in-the-dark arcade.
- Safe: Calm mode, reduced-motion respect, photosensitivity warning.

## MVP vs later
- **MVP:** client-only, no backend. Spotify playback + mic/tab audio → lights.
- **Phase 2:** shared party rooms where friends join one room and lights sync.

## Hard rules
- No user-uploaded audio files.
- No storing or redistributing copyrighted audio; analysis is local/ephemeral.
- Never store Spotify tokens on a server; memory/session only.
- All copy in English, consistently playful and cheeky.
